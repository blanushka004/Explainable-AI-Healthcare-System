Evidence, made visible.

Understand the prediction, not just the score.

Clarity is an explainable AI healthcare research application that estimates heart-disease presence from 13 recorded measurements and shows what influenced the model's result.

Health assessment

Enter 13 recorded measurements from a report to generate an assessment score and risk indicator.

Explainable AI

See which recorded factors raised or lowered the model's prediction using SHAP-based explanations.

Assessment history

Save and securely review previous assessments and their original explanations.

Model transparency

Administrators can inspect model evaluation, thresholds, data lineage, and model versions.

Built with FastAPI, scikit-learn, SHAP, PostgreSQL and SQLAlchemy.

Educational research prototype. Not a medical diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
